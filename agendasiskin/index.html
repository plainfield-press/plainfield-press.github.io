Council and board agendas go up days before the vote - dense PDFs, posted once, easy to miss. AgendaSiskin is testing a keyword alert service for public agendas. The historical Dallas sample below shows what we have actually checked. Automated monitoring and alerts are not yet live.

How it works

1. Name your place + keywords"Maple Street," "short-term rental," "food truck," "zoning" - the words that decide whether a meeting matters to you.
2. Proposed watchCity council, planning commission or county board: the intended service checks new public agenda packets. Coverage must be agreed before any watch starts.
3. Proposed alertThe intended alert identifies the agenda item and meeting date with a source link. An agenda item is not a final decision.
